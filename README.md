# pdt-nhanes

Metadata for the [National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES)](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/), built to the [PublicDataToolkit](https://github.com/AllenDowney/PublicDataToolkit) standard.

**Status:** started from the PublicDataToolkit template; no records yet.

## What the dataset is

NCHS's survey of the health and nutrition of the US civilian noninstitutionalized population, combining interviews with physical examinations and laboratory tests at mobile examination centers. Released in two-year cycles of linked files.

## Terms of use

The terms are at https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/policy/data-user-agreement.html. They haven't been reviewed yet, so nothing built from this repo may ship (STANDARD.md §8). Once a person has decided them, summarize them here; the decision is in [`dataset.yaml`](dataset.yaml).

## How to get the data

The files are listed in [`sources.yaml`](sources.yaml), with where to get each and its checksum.

## How to use the metadata

- `metadata/releases/<release>/variables.jsonl`: one record per variable, with where each fact came from.
- `docs/`: a page per variable, generated by `make views`.
- `agent/`: shards for LLMs; start with `agent/SKILL.md`.

## How to cite

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS). National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey Data. Hyattsville, MD: U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.

## How to rebuild

With the sources in `data/raw/` and a PublicDataToolkit checkout next to this repo:

```bash
make build
make views
make check
```
